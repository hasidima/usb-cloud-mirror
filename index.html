USB cloud mirror

A private utility for its owner to keep local copies of their own cloud files on an encrypted USB disk.

Google Drive access

The Google Drive connection has read-only access. It reads file information and exports Google Docs, Sheets and Slides into Microsoft Office formats. The exported files are written to the owner's encrypted USB disk. It does not change or delete the original files in Google Drive.

This utility runs locally on the owner's computer. It is not a public file-storage service and is not offered for other people to use.

Read the privacy statement for details about data access and storage.
